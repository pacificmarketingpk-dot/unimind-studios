What we do

Four skills under one roof.

Most businesses hire a designer, a developer and an SEO person separately, then spend weeks getting them to agree. We do all four, so nothing gets lost between handovers.

Brand

How your business looks and sounds: logo, colours, fonts and the way you talk to customers.

Websites

Pages planned around what your customer needs to know, then built to load fast.

Product design

Screens for apps and online tools that people can use without reading a manual.

Search

SEO, short for search engine optimisation: the work that helps Google understand and show your pages.

The problem

Most business websites describe the company. Few answer the customer's question.

  • Visitors can't tell what you doThe first screen talks about vision and values. A new visitor leaves before they find your actual service.
  • Pages load slowly on phonesLarge images and heavy code make people wait. Many give up before the page appears.
  • Search engines miss youWithout clear headings and page descriptions, Google struggles to understand who the page is for.
  • Every small change needs a developerUpdating a price or a phone number turns into a ticket, an invoice and a week of waiting.

Why Unimind

How we're different.

Four habits we keep on every project, large or small.

Plain words first

We write the page before we design it. If a sentence would confuse your customer, it doesn't make the cut.

Speed is part of the design

We test on ordinary phones and slow connections, not only on fast office laptops.

One team, start to finish

The people who plan your site also design and build it. Nobody has to explain your business twice.

Honest reporting

No promises about rankings. Each month you see what changed, what worked and what we'll try next.

Process

Questions

Questions we hear often.

Short answers. If yours isn't here, ask us in a meeting.

What does Unimind Studios do?

We design and build brands, websites and digital products. That covers the logo and look of your business, the website itself, the code behind it, and the work that helps people find it on Google.

How long does a new website take?

It depends on how many pages you need and how ready your content is. After our first meeting we send a written timeline with dates for each stage, so you know what happens when before any work starts.

Can you guarantee we will rank first on Google?

No. Nobody can honestly promise that, because Google decides rankings. What we can do is build pages that are fast, clearly written and easy for search engines to read, then show you exactly what changed each month.

Can you improve our current website instead of starting over?

Yes. We start with a review of what you have. Sometimes a few fixed pages and a faster setup are enough. If a rebuild makes more sense, we explain why in plain terms.

Who owns the website when it is finished?

You do. At the end of the project we hand over the files, the logins and the source code, which is the underlying text that makes the site work.

How do we get started?

Book a meeting using the Book Meeting button. We ask about your business, your customers and what is not working today. Then we send a written plan and a quote.

Next step

Tell us what isn't working.

A short meeting, no slides. You'll leave with a clearer idea of what to fix first, whether or not we work together.
