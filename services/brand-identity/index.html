Brand identity

A brand people recognise at a glance.

We design how your business looks and sounds, then write it down in a short guide so everyone who works with you keeps it consistent.

Book MeetingWhat's included
Short answer

What is brand identity?

Brand identity is everything that makes your business recognisable: your logo, colours, fonts, images and the way you write. A good identity looks the same on your website, your invoices and your social posts, so people remember you and trust you sooner.

The challenge

Why most brands feel patchy.

A logo made in a hurry, a website in different colours, social posts in a third style. Customers notice, even when they can't say why.

  • ×Your logo looks blurry or cramped on phones
  • ×Every designer uses different colours and fonts
  • ×Your writing changes tone from page to page
  • ×You look smaller or older than you really are
Overview

One clear system instead of loose pieces.

We start by understanding who your customers are and how you want them to feel. Then we design a small set of parts that work together and write simple rules for using them.

The goal is not a fancy logo. It is a brand your team can use correctly without asking us every time.

What it does for you

  • People recognise you faster
  • New materials take less time to make
  • Your business looks as established as it is
  • Designers and printers get clear instructions

Process

Questions

Brand identity questions.

Do you only design logos?

No. A logo is one part of a brand. We also choose colours and fonts, set the tone of voice and give you templates, so the logo is used well.

Can you keep parts of our current brand?

Yes. If people already know your logo or colours, we can modernise them instead of starting over. We discuss this in the first workshop.

Will we own the logo?

You will own the final logo and brand files once the project is paid in full. The exact terms are written in our agreement.
