Website care

Keep your website safe, fast and up to date.

A monthly plan that handles updates, backups and small changes, with a short report so you always know the state of your site.

Book MeetingWhat's included
Short answer

What is website care?

Website care is ongoing upkeep for a site that is already live. It covers software updates, security, backups, checking the site stays online and making small content changes, so problems are caught early instead of after something breaks.

The challenge

Why websites quietly decay.

A site that nobody looks after slowly gets slower, less secure and out of date. Problems often appear at the worst moment.

  • ×Plug-ins and themes go months without updates
  • ×There is no recent backup if something breaks
  • ×Small text changes wait weeks
  • ×Nobody notices when a form stops working
Overview

Small, regular care instead of big repairs.

We check, update and back up your site on a set schedule, handle small edits from your team and send a short report every month.

Care plans work best for sites we built, but we can take over sites built by others after a check-up.

What it does for you

  • Lower risk of hacks and outages
  • A recent backup is always ready
  • Quick turnaround on small edits
  • A clear monthly picture of your site

Questions

Website care questions.

Can you look after a site you didn't build?

Yes, after a check-up. We first review the site so we know what we're taking on and can flag any risks.

What happens to unused edit hours?

This depends on the plan you choose and is written in the agreement.

Can we cancel the plan?

The notice period is set out in the agreement. We make sure you have all files and logins if you leave.
