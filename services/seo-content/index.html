Short answer

What is SEO?

SEO stands for search engine optimisation. It is the work that helps search engines like Google understand your pages and show them to people searching for what you offer. It includes clear writing, a fast and tidy website, and earning links from other trusted sites.

The challenge

Why good businesses stay invisible.

Search engines can only rank what they can read and understand. Many sites hide their best answers behind vague headings and slow pages.

  • ×Pages don't match what people actually search for
  • ×Titles and descriptions are missing or repeated
  • ×The site is slow or hard for Google to read
  • ×Nobody reports what changed or why
Overview

Clear answers, tidy foundations, honest reports.

We fix the technical basics first, then improve and write pages that answer the questions your customers search. We also make answers easy for AI assistants to quote.

No one can promise a ranking position. We focus on the work that makes ranking possible and show you what changes.

What it does for you

  • Pages that match real searches

  • A site Google can read easily

Questions

SEO and content questions.

How long until we see results?

SEO usually takes months rather than weeks, and it depends on your field and competition. We show progress each month so you can judge for yourself.

Do you guarantee first place on Google?

No. Google decides rankings, so any guarantee would be dishonest. We focus on the work that gives your pages the best chance.

What are AEO and GEO?

AEO means answer engine optimisation and GEO means generative engine optimisation. Both are about making your content easy for AI assistants and answer boxes to understand and quote.
