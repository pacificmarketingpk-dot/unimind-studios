Website design

Website design that answers your customer first.

We plan every page around the questions your customers ask, then design layouts you can click through and comment on before anything is built.

Book MeetingWhat's included
Short answer

What is website design?

Website design is planning what goes on each page and how it looks: the order of information, the headings, the images and the buttons. Good design makes it obvious what you do and what the visitor should do next, on a phone as well as a laptop.

The challenge

Why good businesses get weak websites.

Many sites are designed around the company's org chart instead of the customer's questions. The result looks fine but doesn't help anyone decide.

  • ×The first screen doesn't say what you do
  • ×Important pages are hard to find in the menu
  • ×The site looks cramped or broken on phones
  • ×Visitors read but don't contact you
Overview

Words first, then layout, then style.

We write the key messages and map the pages before we pick colours. That way the design supports what you need to say, instead of decorating empty boxes.

You see clickable drafts early, so changes happen while they are cheap, not after the site is built.

What it does for you

  • Visitors understand you in seconds
  • Clear next steps on every page
  • Layouts that work on any screen
  • Fewer surprises during the build

Process

How it works, step by step.

You approve each step before the next one starts.

  1. 01

    Plan

  2. 02

  3. 03

    Design

Questions

Website design questions.

Do we need to write all the text ourselves?

No. We draft the key messages from what you tell us, and you correct anything that isn't right. You know your business, we know how to make it clear.

Can you design a site we build ourselves?

Yes. We can hand over organised design files that your own developer can build from.

How many changes can we ask for?

The number of review rounds is agreed in the quote. Most changes happen during the wireframe stage, when they take the least time.
